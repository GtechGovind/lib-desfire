# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Treat each other with respect in issues, discussions, reviews and other project
spaces. Give specific, constructive feedback, assume good intent and make room
for questions and disagreement about technical decisions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, personal
attacks and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.
Do not share credentials, personal card data or protected traffic.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abuse through [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
For project moderation, contact [@GtechGovind](https://github.com/GtechGovind)
through an available private contact method; a public request for private contact
should contain no incident details or personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants, restrict interaction
or ban accounts from project spaces according to the severity and repetition of
the behavior. Reports will be handled as privately as the available channels
allow. Maintainers are also expected to follow this policy.
